About the Conference

The emergence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and its progressively wider impact on many sectors requires an assessment of its impact on the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals. AI is aiming to provide the next generation product developments to reduce their environmental and social impact by improving efficiency with sustainability. In this context, GMR Institute of Technology intends to organize an international conference titled “AI Powered Technology Integration for Sustainability’’(AI-PTIS-2024). This conference provides a global platform for academicians, researchers, industry professionals, and research scholars to share their respective research findings, discuss advancements, and address challenges across various fields (cross-functional) for AI integrated sustainable developments. Through presentations, discussions, and networking opportunities, participants can exchange knowledge and foster collaborations. The conference promotes interdisciplinary collaboration and aims to connect academia, industry, and policymakers. AI-PTIS-2024 will be a two-day conference held in July 2024 in a hybrid mode, catering to the convenience of researchers and inspiring technological innovations towards integration of AI for sustainable industrial growth.


About GMR Institute of Technology

GMRIT is an Autonomous Engineering college established in the year 1997 by GMR Varalakshmi Foundation – the corporate social responsibility arm of GMR Group. Located in Rajam, Vizianagaram district of Andhra Pradesh, GMRIT provides its learning community state-of-the-art facilities, infrastructure, and a competent faculty. GMRIT was ranked 188th in NIRF-2022 and ranked among the top 5 colleges in the state of Andhra Pradesh and top 50 colleges in the country by popular education magazines. The institute is accredited by both NAAC (with A grade) for the three consecutive cycle till 2025. All the UG programs ae accredited by the NBA under Tier I (4th cycle). GMRIT offers 8 UG and 6 PG programs and delivering quality technical education for the all-round development of engineering aspirants. With a strong belief that Academics and Research has to go hand in hand, GMRIT promotes research by organizing conferences and capacity building program in all the emerging areas. The Institute is also positioned globally in "THE" Impact Rankings 201-300 Rank Band in SDG 6, 301-400 in SDG 7 and 401-600 in SDG 1 and 2.
